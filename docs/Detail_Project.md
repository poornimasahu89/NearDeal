LOCAL COMMERCE & SMART BARGAINING PLATFORM
Project Concept, Business Model & Website Specification
Search Local. Compare Smart. Negotiate Better. Buy with Confidence.
1. Executive Summary
The proposed platform is a location-based e-commerce marketplace designed to connect customers with nearby sellers while giving customers the ability to compare products, prices, reviews, seller ratings, distance, availability, and negotiation options in one place.
Unlike traditional e-commerce platforms where prices are generally fixed, this platform will introduce a built-in bargaining and negotiation system. Customers can make offers directly to sellers, while sellers can accept, reject, or counter-offer.
Overall shopping experience:
Search → Discover Nearby Sellers → Check Price → Negotiate → Purchase → Review
2. Problem Statement
Customers shopping locally often face several difficulties.
•	Visit multiple stores.
•	Call different sellers to ask for prices.
•	Compare products manually.
•	Compare seller reputation.
•	Travel from one store to another.
•	Negotiate separately with each seller.
•	Determine whether a seller's quoted price is reasonable.
•	Spend significant time finding the best combination of price, quality, and seller reliability.
At the same time, local sellers may struggle to reach customers outside their immediate physical location. There is an opportunity to combine the convenience of online product discovery with the flexibility of local shopping.
3. Proposed Solution

16. Smart Bargaining System
The bargaining system is the primary differentiating feature. A seller can choose whether a product is:
•	Negotiable
•	Limited Negotiation
•	Fixed Price
For negotiable products, the customer will see:
Listed Price: ₹28,000
Your Offer: ₹24,000
The customer submits the offer. The seller receives the offer and can:
•	Accept
•	Reject
•	Counter Offer
17. Negotiation Example
Initial Listing — Seller price: ₹30,000
Customer — Offers: ₹25,000
Seller — Counter-offers: ₹28,000
Customer — Counter-offers: ₹26,500
Seller — Accepts: ₹26,500
Final transaction price: ₹26,500
18. Negotiation Rules
•	Minimum offer limits.
•	Maximum number of counter-offers.
•	Offer expiration.
•	Seller-defined negotiation range.
•	Automatic offer rejection below a seller's configured minimum.
For example, a seller may list a product at ₹30,000 and privately configure a minimum acceptable price of ₹26,000. The seller's minimum price should remain private.

30. Business Model
The primary revenue model will be a 2% transaction commission charged to the seller on every successfully completed purchase made through the platform. Customers will not be charged this commission as part of the core model.
31. 2% Seller Commission
The commission will be calculated based on the final purchase price after negotiation.
Item	Amount
Product listed price	₹30,000
Final negotiated price	₹27,000
Platform commission (2%)	₹540
Seller receives	₹26,460
Platform revenue	₹540

Revenue Formula: Final Transaction Value × 2%
32. Revenue Examples
Monthly Transaction Value	Platform Revenue at 2%
₹1,00,000	₹2,000
₹5,00,000	₹10,000
₹10,00,000	₹20,000
₹50,00,000	₹1,00,000
₹1 Crore	₹2,00,000

The business therefore grows as the total value of transactions conducted through the platform grows.
33. Why the 2% Model Works
The platform's interests are aligned with sellers and customers. If sellers receive more customers and complete more transactions, the platform generates more revenue.
More transactions → Higher seller sales → Higher platform revenue
The platform does not need to depend entirely on advertising. The core business model is tied directly to successful transactions.
